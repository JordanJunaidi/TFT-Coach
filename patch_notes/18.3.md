# Teamfight Tactics patch 18.3

Source: https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/teamfight-tactics-patch-18-3/

Buff struggling rerollers, squash bugs (not you Kha'Zix), and Enchant the Wilds!
18.3 is a medium-sized patch focused on giving new life to comps that may have appeared during PBE but haven't been found in the Wilds since—I'm looking at you Wolf Bros. Past balance, we've got a ton of bug fixes for this patch, split into two categories—gameplay, where we finally found those missing PvE fights, and stability/performance, where we added a fix to the Augment UI that could result in selecting an incorrect Augment. Oh, and we also have a few Wisp nerfs to the must-buys and buffs for our often-skipped Combat Augments. Let's get into it!
Rodger "Riot Prism" Caudill
Katie "Riot Ukime" Guo

## MID-PATCH UPDATE

### SEPTEMBER 28TH
Over the weekend, a new strategy appeared that allowed players to consistently achieve two-star 5-costs far earlier than intended. This strategy was made possible due to bugs with the Greater Polymorph Wisp. We've temporarily disabled this until 18.4, when we can fully implement the intended bugfixes.
- Major Polymorph has been temporarily disabled

### SEPTEMBER 24TH
Our 18.3 B patch is both to respond to later meta changes that weren’t covered with the initial patch and to fix a few balance changes that were reverted unintentionally from 18.2 B to 18.3.

### 18.3 B PATCH BALANCE CHANGES
A few meta evolutions slipped through our patch notes after we locked our balance changes for 18.3.
- Blackthorn AP Sacrifice Base Damage Amp: 14% ⇒ 12%
- Kha’Zix Ability Base Damage: 285/400/580 AP ⇒ 265/370/535 AP
- Kha’Zix Ability Isolation Damage: 310/445/660 ⇒ 285/410/605 AP
- Brambleback cast time is now slightly faster.

### 18.2 CHAMPION TARGETING
With 18.3, we intentionally reverted a subtle Champion Targeting change from 18.2. Our goal is complete parity in champion targeting between Sets 17 and 18, but there have been bugs that have altered melee targeting at the start of combat. An attempted fix to other movement bugs caused unintended effects in 18.2. We’ve reverted that change and are investigating a better way to return parity to targeting.

### 18.2 B BALANCE CHANGES RE-ADDED
When we went from 18.2B to 18.3 a few balance changes accidentally reverted. We’re reenabling them as listed below.
- Camille Ability Damage: 160/240/410/700 AD ⇒ 150/225/375/640 AD

- Ashe Trail Duration: 4s ⇒ 3s
- Ashe DoT Base Damage Per Second (AD): 5/8 AD ⇒ 9/14 AD
- Draven Bounty Hunter:
  - Casts for 4-costs: 5 ⇒ 6
  - Reward for 8 Casts: 10 Rerolls ⇒ 6 Rerolls
  - Attack for 5-cost: 50 ⇒ 60
  - Damage for 7 Gold: 8,000 ⇒ 10,000
  - Kills for 12 Gold: 6 ⇒ 8

### AUGMENTS TEMPORARILY DISABLED

### TRAITS
As a splash with powerful units, Blossom (3) provides too much value for a few over-performing Wisps in particular. This change may look like a big nerf, cause there are a lot of lines, but the reality of gameplay here is that you'll typically only be impacted by 2-5 of these Wisp nerfs throughout a game in most cases.
As a high-stakes trait without the potential to be griefed, we released Coven with cashout generation that was more conservative than past high-stakes traits. We're increasing Loss Essence generation across most breakpoints here to bring Coven out of the dark.
Hunter, Defender, Invoker, and even Inferno nerfs are to the highest breakpoint of the traits, where powerful Emblems allow for too much value. We have a few more Emblem specific nerfs later on, but so as to not make the max breakpoints, nor the Emblems, useless, we've split the nerfs into two smaller parts.

### UNITS: TIER 1
It's about time for Karma to get what she deserves: a buff across all star levels, but especially three-star, where she's yet to be seen as a mid-game reroll carry.
Unlike Karma, Ornn has been able to access Artifacts too easily with optimal set-ups. We're slowing three-star Ornn down, since he's been able to print these Artifacts for Solar comps to spike in the late game, while making sure our two-star Ornns ornn't griefed.
As a reroll carry, Veigar has found success with a few openers that can shore up his weak early game. Some of these set-ups lead to incredibly powerful Veigar carries that feel almost impossible to overcome, but if you want to add Veigar in for his traits, he's all but a dead unit. We're trimming a bit out of his power ceiling, so Veigar can be less of a dead unit when not played as the centerpiece of a comp. There's certainly three to eight potential short jokes for this context paragraph, but I feel like it's about time we stood above that. #shortkings(and queens)
- Karma Explosion Damage: 120/180/270/460 AP ⇒ 125/185/300/515 AP
- Ornn three-star Forge Power Bonus: +100% ⇒ +85%
- Ornn 2nd Artifact Threshold: 155,000 Forge Power ⇒ 140,000 Forge Power
- Veigar AP Per Kill: 3% ⇒ 2%
- Veigar Ability Damage Base: 175/265/395/670 AP ⇒ 200/300/450/765 AP

### UNITS: TIER 2
Alistar hasn't been much more than a big health bar when three-starred. He's getting a lot more damage in his ability and a more powerful heal to have this Elder bull feel like a younger pup.
Speaking of pups, the Wolf Bros are back to be your new favorite Level 6 reroll comp to play. Just remember to sacrifice an Attack Fighter and not a Tank for the Blackthorn Hex, and since we haven't seen Warwick in a while, be sure to grab a Titan's Resolve and Spear of Shojin, cause mana regen = ramping Attack Speed. As for Murkwolf, positioning, positioning, positioning… for you and your opponent.
Gromp is also getting a buff that should be more impactful to the AD form, but a nice buff to AP as well. Gromp and Murkwolf being buffed in the same patch may seem scary, since Riftbeast is not too far off, but most Riftbeast comps do not run both of these Units, especially as carries. Maybe that can change though?

### UNITS: TIER 3
With 6 Defender being nerfed, which was a huge source of stall power for Cassiopeia comps, we can put some power into Cassiopeia and Rammus directly so they can slither/roll into more than just 6 Defender comps.
Even in his prime, AP flex, tanky Blackthorn sacrifice, two-star Malphite era, Azir was never the go-to carry.
- Azir Ability Damage: 43/65/103 ⇒ 46/69/110
- Cassiopeia Ability Damage: 400/600/950 ⇒ 425/630/1020
- Rammus Shield: 350/450/550 ⇒ 400/550/725

### UNITS: TIER 4
Lillia's only go-to comps, Fae (4) + Cassiopeia Defender, aren't typically seen past 4th place, leaving this 4-cost tank without a home. With a solid buff to her healing, she may be able to survive enough for a second cast, sleeping more foes, and making her way into more comps as a utility tank.
Nidalee's AD form was gaining more Armor penetration than intended, but even with that fixed, she's still slightly over the line, so we've got a small nerf to the AD scaling on her swipe. And while AD Nidalee is our strongest 4-cost at the moment, AP Nidalee is rarely itemized, so we're giving her third attack, her snipe at the farthest enemy with the least items, a pointy buff.
- Lillia Heal: 325/475 AP ⇒ 350/525 AP
- Nidalee: Fixed a bug where Nidalee was gaining 60% Armor Penetration instead of 40% Armor Penetration
- Nidalee AD form Ability Armor Penetration: 40% ⇒ 60% (no functional change)
- Nidalee AD form Ability Damage: 225/340 AD ⇒ 210/315 AD
- Nidalee AP Form Third Attack Damage: 300/450 AP ⇒ 330/500 AP

### UNITS: TIER 5
Even when we tried to incentivize itemizing Taric last patch, he was still often skipped over by our premiere 4-cost tanks (or Maokai). We're polishing the Emerald Aspect's heal to make a bit of AP feel good on him, while taking away some of his passive team-wide shielding (which is a flat value unaffected by items).
- Lux Solar Lux Bonus Damage per 3-star: 12% ⇒ 15%
- Taric Passive Shield Base Value: 100/225 ⇒ 75/150
- Taric Heal: 250/375 AP ⇒ 275/450 AP

### ARTIFACTS
Draven with the Collector is absolutely terrifying, and it still will be, just a tiny bit less. But past Draven (and Aphelios), the Collector provides a lot of econ and combat power, making it one of the better Artifacts on the set, especially in the right hands.
Unlike The Collector, Statikk Shiv has yet to find consistent users apart from Kayle. A bit more Ability Power should make this item more appealing even in situations where it's dropped too late in the game to field Kayle.
- The Collector AD: 40% ⇒ 35%
- Eternal Pact Mana on Tank Ability Cast: 15 ⇒ 10
- Statikk Shiv Base Ability Power: 15% ⇒ 25%

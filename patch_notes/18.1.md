## Mid-Patch Updates

### AUGUST 31ST AND SEPTEMBER 1ST
Our third mid-patch update will be rolling out August 31st for the balance changes and September 1st for bug fixes and quality of life improvements.

### TRAITS
Riftbeast (7) has proven to be a super consistent trait whether you’re sitting there to star up your 1-costs, or pushing levels to slot in flexible Legendary units.

### CHAMPIONS
Drop Pebbles, Cinderling stocks are on fire! Through optimized builds, and consistency of starring this little guy up via Riftbeast shops, Cinderling’s ramped up as a powerful carry. We’ve got a base AD and ability nerf to keep him in check and give Pebbles fans a more equal footing.
Cassiopeia Defender is one of the most consistent comps of our patch. We’re taking a big chunk out of the main damage threat here.
Master Yi is another reroll star that’s become a powerhouse, especially with an Artifact or two. We’re lowering his defenses to keep this melee-carry burstable.
Amumu has had a lot to cry about with our release patch. Let’s cheer him up with a double buff.
Draven’s our worst performing 5-cost by a considerable amount. We’re swinging big with a buff to allow him to cast much sooner and take advantage of more bleeds being built up. Attack Speed, which was already his best stat, is going to be even better, so grab those bows!

### GAME FEEL AND PERFORMANCE
Note that these changes will be patched separately tomorrow, September first in the early evening. Alongside a few gameplay-related bugfixes we also have a quality of life, or game feel, adjustment for dragging and dropping units to allow for more reactive/snappier unit movement. This should be most noticeable when using the units on your bench as a virtual fidget spinner.

Impact may vary depending on your device specs, but we'll continue to roll out fixes over the next few patches to continue improving here.

## ENCHANTED WILDS RELEASE PERFORMANCE NOTES
Patch 18.1 is our first foray into the Enchanted Wilds on live with our transition from Hextech to Unreal. With the new engine, it's taking time for us to get up to speed, reach parity, and eventually, accelerate into the full potential of our new engine. With that in mind, there are going to be some very noticeable differences between set 17 and 18 that we are actively working to close the gap on.
- Bugs: With a new set going live, there are always a few bugs to be found with the millions of games occurring in just the opening hours of a set. With a new engine, we expect that few to be more than usual, but as with PBE, we're actively working on fixing them, so as always please report any bugs you come across using community social spaces, support tickets, or the client.
- Loading Times: Upon launch, loading into Enchanted Wilds will take longer than normal. We thank you for your patience during these load screens, and we are working on the back-end to reduce them.
- Pings and Mini-map: You may notice that the mini-map is gone, and with it, pings are MIA. With the minimap gone, we still want to preserve the strategic (or humorous) communications from Pings, so we're taking Pings into the shop to rework them into a social tool that's not bound to the minimap. This will take some time and won't be ready on release, but we'll ping you when we've got a clearer understanding of their release timing.
- Friend spectate on Live is something that we know is important for community super-contributors, community events, and just hanging out with your pals. It’s something we will not have on launch, but is on the docket to release long-term with the intention of a better spectator experience overall.
- Patch Size: 18.1 is going to be big on the data size side of things, and the game side of things—it is a new set… Expect future patches to be smaller in size after our initial release.
- Finishers: As we port our entire catalogue of Tacticians, Arenas, Booms, and Emotes over to Unreal, the trickiest aspect of our transition has been bringing over Finishers. As a temporary solution to speed up this process, we’re displaying Finishers as a video rather than in engine. Until Finishers are rebuilt in engine, they’ll be performed on River Sprites.
- Cosmetics: Migrating all our cosmetics over from Hextech to Unreal is going to take some time. Each patch we plan on adding more and more cosmetics until we’ve achieved parity. For a detailed list of the Cosmetics available on release, please see the section at the end of these notes.

## SYSTEMS

### SET MECHANIC: WISPS
For a detailed dive into Wisps mechanic make sure you check out the Enchanted Wilds Overview. For now, here are some highlights.
- Wisps are split into 7 Categories (Champion, Combat, Misc, Shop, Gold/XP, Risky, Item). These categories have distinct coloring and icons to make rolling down and scouting as their icon will appear by a player's Tactician HP bar upon purchase.
- Wisps can only be purchased during the planning phase. After the planning phase, the Wisp will be whisked away, revealing the unit that was hiding behind it.
- Wisps appear in every other shop.
- After stage 5, every other Wisp is guaranteed to be a Combat Wisp.

### CAROUSEL
- The Carousel has returned!
- Carousel now has a chance to contain more champions and/or champions with a higher costs than normal.

### TARGETING
In hextech, units were hardcoded to retarget after being CC'd (stunned, knocked-up), targeting the closest enemy to them. This meant if a backliner was hit by an AOE stun, they'd retarget whoever is closest to them'bad luck if you're an assassin that's snuck into the backline. With Set 18, we're able to do away with this awkward retargeting as CC will no longer be accompanied by target amnesia.
- Units will no longer retarget after returning from Crowd Control.

### PVE LOOT
Pro-tip: You still shouldn't miss loot at Stage 4-7.
- Missed loot from Stage 4-7, or later PVE rounds, can now overflow to the next PVE round.

### OPENING ENCOUNTERS
We're removing a few Opening Encounters that were too warping, while adding some more standard-style Encounters to keep lower impact variance varied. We've played with high and low impact Opening Encounters for a while now, and, especially in a set like 18, there doesn't need to be such extreme highs in variance with this system, as much of the game to game variance comes instead from Wisps, Augments, Item distribution, and what units you see in the shop.
- New Encounters:
  - Completed Item Anvil Start: All players start with a Completed Item Anvil.
  - Reroll Subscription: Gain rerolls at the start of each stage.
  - Item Forge: All players start with 2 Completed Item Anvils.
- Removed Encounters:
  - Loot Subscription
  - Artifact Anvil
  - Reroll Start
